boothlord-pump-fun-sprint-entry
================================

Launch scalper for the pump.fun paper-trading replay (agent-fun/replay/v2): small (≤0.05 SOL, capped by the 1%-of-real-reserve size cap) early stakes on young coins past 1 SOL of real reserve with real net buying and at least one non-creator buyer, no big runup already in the price. Losers are cut at -40%, winners are run with a trailing stop (armed at +20% of cost, 10% fall from peak value), and anything still open after 1000 slots or at curve completion is flattened. One buy per coin, no averaging down, no shorts, no leverage.

Every reply is a pure function of the hello + batch stream: no clock, no randomness, no network, no file reads outside the repo, so the two graded executions produce byte-identical action logs.
